The Zen of Tik

Maya decides what a node is.
TikMaya decides how to touch it.
Trigger decides why it matters.

Do not argue with the host.
Do not leak meaning upward.
Let each layer breathe its own air.

Wrap behavior, not identity.
Reveal structure, do not invent it.
A wrapper explains. It does not rule.

Inheritance is for is.
Composition is for has.
When in doubt, add less ancestry.

Rules live where they act.
Never lower. Rarely higher.
Gravity belongs close to the ground.

If something moves,
parents, constrains, or remembers where it is,
it is Transform-centric.

If something defines form,
surface, volume, or silhouette,
it is Shape-centric.

Never confuse motion with meaning.
Never confuse geometry with intent.

APIs should feel smaller than the system beneath them.
Power comes from restraint.

If behavior surprises the reader,
it is in the wrong layer.

Names are contracts.
Aliases are kindness.
Ambiguity is technical debt wearing a smile.

Expose the obvious.
Hide the inevitable.
Document the sharp edges.

Prefer resolution over reach.
Prefer wrapping over rewriting.
Prefer clarity over cleverness.

If Maya already knows the truth,
ask it.
If it does not, do not pretend.

The graph is sacred.
Side effects are confessions.
Global state is a ghost story.

Tools should compose like sentences,
not shout like monologues.

A good abstraction disappears.
A bad one demands attention.

Leave space for rigs you have not met yet.
Leave exits for decisions you may regret.

Simple things should be simple.
Complex things should be possible.
Magical things should be explicit.

Now import tik.
Read slowly.
Then build with intent.
